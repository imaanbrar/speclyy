# Vision

## Vision Statement
Build the **trusted product operating system for interior designers** — a fast, structured, and export-ready workspace that turns scattered product research into a clean, reliable project specification package.

## What We’re Building
A product + project system where designers can:
- Capture products quickly (manual entry or paste a product URL)
- Organize products into a project (by room, zone, or category)
- Rely on consistent, structured fields (brand, collection, finish, SKU, dimensions, links, notes)
- Export polished schedules (FFE/spec sheets) for clients, builders, and trades
- Contribute high-quality items to a shared global inventory **without polluting it**

## North Star Outcomes
### For Designers
- **Speed:** Reduce hours of product hunting + formatting into minutes
- **Confidence:** Fewer ordering/spec errors because data is structured and reviewable
- **Reuse:** Build a personal library that compounds value across projects
- **Professional delivery:** One-click exports that look consistent and “builder-ready”

### For the Ecosystem (Builders / Trades / Clients)
- **Clarity:** Clean schedules with correct SKUs/finishes and fewer back-and-forth questions
- **Fewer change orders:** Less rework caused by wrong specs or missing details
- **Smoother coordination:** Everyone references the same source of truth

## Core Principles
1. **Trust First**
   - A library is only valuable if it’s complete and accurate.
   - Missing obvious finishes/SKUs undermines confidence immediately.

2. **Structured by Default**
   - Products must resolve into consistent entities:
     **Brand → Collection → Product → Finish/Variant → SKU**
   - Clean data beats pretty UI.

3. **Designer-Speed Input**
   - Adding a product should feel instant:
     - Paste URL → prefill fields (when possible)
     - Manual entry always works (no blockers)

4. **Export is the Product**
   - The “deliverable” is not a board or a list — it’s a **builder/client-ready schedule**.
   - Export should be easy, consistent, and customizable.

5. **Global Inventory With Quality Control**
   - Designers can create project items freely.
   - Promotion to global inventory requires review to prevent duplicates and garbage.

## What Success Looks Like (Future State)
A designer can open a new project, add products in hours (not weeks), and produce a professional FFE/spec package that:
- Has complete finish options and SKUs
- Is consistent across categories and vendors
- Survives revisions and substitutions without breaking
- Becomes a reusable asset for future projects

## What We Are Not (At Least Initially)
- Not a procurement platform
- Not a marketplace
- Not a full accounting/PM tool
- Not a “pretty inspiration board” without structured data behind it

## Target User (Initial Beachhead)
- Freelance interior designers and small studios in **North America**
- Focused on **high-end residential** projects where quality, accuracy, and coordination matter most
- Already building schedules in spreadsheets and PDFs — and frustrated by rework

## North Star Metric
**Time-to-export a client-ready spec schedule** (from first product captured to export-ready deliverable), while maintaining high data completeness (finishes/SKUs).

# Future Vision (AI + Integrations)

## Why This Matters
Once the foundation is strong — **structured products + trusted inventory + exports** — we unlock a step-change: AI can accelerate *creative assembly* and *draft documentation* without sacrificing accuracy.

The future vision expands the product from “spec operating system” into a **design-to-deliver workflow**, where inspiration becomes structured, spec-ready output.

## Future Capabilities

### 1) AI-Generated Moodboards (From Structured Products)
- Generate moodboards automatically from:
  - Project goals (style, budget tier, material preferences)
  - Room type (kitchen, primary ensuite, living room)
  - Existing selections in the project
- Provide multiple directions (“Option A / B / C”) with:
  - Coherent palettes (tone + material harmony)
  - Complementary product suggestions (lighting + hardware + tile)
  - Clear rationale (“why these go together”)

**Key constraint:** AI moodboards are **grounded in real products** from the inventory, not fictional items.

### 2) Pinterest Import → Draft Moodboard + Product Candidates
- Import Pinterest boards (links/images) and automatically:
  - Extract visual themes (colors/materials/forms)
  - Identify likely categories (tile, lighting, faucets, hardware)
  - Suggest real product matches from inventory
  - Create a “Draft Moodboard” with:
    - Inspiration images
    - Proposed product candidates
    - Confidence score + “missing info” flags

### 3) Draft Spec Sheets From Inspiration (AI-Assisted, Human-Approved)
- Convert early-stage inspiration into a **Draft Spec Sheet**:
  - Room-by-room structure
  - Suggested categories per room
  - Placeholder fields for unknowns
  - Auto-populated product candidates when matches exist
- Add review workflow:
  - “Needs confirmation” (finish/SKU/dimensions)
  - “Ready for export”
  - “Alternate selected”

### 4) Canva Export (Client-Ready Presentation Packs)
- Export project selections and moodboards directly to Canva:
  - Pre-built Canva templates (brandable)
  - Auto-fill pages by room/category
  - Include images, key specs, links, and notes
- Enable designers to deliver:
  - Beautiful client decks
  - Consistent branded presentations
  - Without copy/paste chaos

### 5) Multi-Source Ingestion Beyond URLs
- Expand capture methods:
  - PDF tear sheets (semi-structured extraction)
  - Vendor quote line-items
  - Spreadsheet imports (normalize into structured products)
- Keep the same rule:
  - **Project items can be messy**
  - **Global inventory requires review**

## Future Workflow (End-to-End)
1. Import Pinterest board → system creates draft moodboard + identifies categories  
2. AI proposes real product candidates (grounded in inventory)  
3. Designer approves and edits finishes/SKUs  
4. Export:
   - Builder-ready spec sheet
   - Canva client presentation pack  
5. Approved products are promoted into the global inventory after review

## Future North Star
**From inspiration → export-ready package** in days, not weeks — while maintaining trust and accuracy.
