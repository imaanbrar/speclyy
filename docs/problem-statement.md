# Problem Statement

## Context
Interior designers and small studios deliver client projects by assembling hundreds (often thousands) of products across categories like plumbing, lighting, tile, hardware, appliances, paint, and millwork. The work isn’t just selecting items — it’s **finding accurate product data**, confirming **finish/variant options**, tracking **availability/alternates**, and producing clean, shareable schedules (FFE/spec sheets) for clients, builders, and trades.

Today, this workflow is fragmented across:
- Vendor websites and product pages
- PDFs (spec sheets, tear sheets, catalogs)
- Showroom emails, quotes, and line-item lists
- Pinterest/Instagram references with missing details
- Spreadsheets that break as soon as products change
- Disconnected tools that don’t reliably map **Brand → Collection → Product → Finish/Variant → SKU**

## The Core Problem
Designers cannot trust product data.

Product information is often:
- **Unstructured** (PDFs, images, marketing pages)
- **Incomplete** (missing finishes, missing SKUs, unclear dimensions)
- **Inconsistent** (different naming across vendors, duplicates, outdated specs)
- **Hard to reuse** (every project rebuilds the same product library from scratch)
- **Hard to export** (turning research into a client-ready schedule is manual and error-prone)

## Consequences
This creates three costly outcomes:
1. **Time Waste**  
   Hours are lost searching, copying, formatting, and re-checking product details across multiple sources.
2. **Expensive Mistakes**  
   Wrong finish ordered, incorrect SKU, outdated spec shared, or incorrect dimensions — leading to delays and change orders.
3. **Loss of Trust in Tools**  
   If a product library is missing obvious items or finish options, designers quickly dismiss the system as unreliable.

## Why This Matters (North America, High-End Residential)
Premium residential projects amplify the problem because they involve:
- High customization and frequent revisions
- Multiple vendors/showrooms and many stakeholders
- Tight coordination with builders and trades
- Substitutions due to lead times, discontinued items, and availability shifts

Small data errors can cascade into schedule slips, rework, and client frustration.

## Opportunity
There is a clear gap for a designer-focused system that:
- Produces **clean, structured, trustworthy product data**
- Lets designers **add products quickly** (manual entry + paste a URL)
- Supports **project-level exports** (FFE/spec sheets) without spreadsheet chaos
- Grows a **global inventory** by promoting only **reviewed, high-quality** products (avoiding duplicates/garbage)

## One-Sentence Problem Statement
Designers spend too much time and take too much risk because product information is fragmented, unstructured, and unreliable—making it difficult to build a trusted, reusable library and generate accurate project spec schedules.
